01BSc thesisValidated platform

Addis Ababa University - Advisor: Dr. Biniam Tadesse

Wearable ECG Platform for Remote Cardiac Telemetry

A four-layer wearable platform combining an STM32L452RET6, ADS1292R analog front end, LIS3DH motion sensor, microSD logging, rechargeable USB-C power, and LTE Cat-1 telemetry.

Evidence

  • Validated ECG acquisition, local logging, and batch telemetry at 250 samples/s.
  • Completed a three-hour LTE/SD soak test with 339 device-confirmed HTTPS uploads and 847,500 ECG/motion samples.
  • Recovered from transient LTE/TLS failures while maintaining approximately 249-250 samples/s.
  • Research-only evaluation reached 91.6% AF recall and 82.4% three-rhythm macro F1.
