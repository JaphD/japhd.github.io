Selected work

BSc thesis · Tested prototype

TirtaTrace

My thesis teammate and I built a wearable platform that records ECG and motion data. I designed two revisions of the four-layer PCB in Altium and implemented timestamped microSD logging in STM32 firmware.

339 device-confirmed uploads in a three-hour LTE and microSD bench test.

  • Dashboard demo
  • Project repository
Hardware revisions and test details

Rev 1 ran on a LiPo battery, used USB only for charging, and used power gating for the LTE modem. Rev 2 ran directly from USB without power gating and used cleaner PCB routing for bench testing.

The telemetry test logged 847,500 ECG and motion samples and demonstrated recovery from transient LTE and TLS failures.

I led PCB design for low-cost air-quality sensing nodes, supported local fabrication, and worked with the firmware and mechanical teams to validate the assembled units.
